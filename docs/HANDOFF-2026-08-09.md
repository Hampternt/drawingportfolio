### Where worktrees live — settled 2026-08-09

**Long-lived worktrees go outside the repo**, at
`~/projects/drawingportfolio.worktrees/<name>`. That is the convention.

The reason is `.claude/worktrees/` is subject to exit-time keep/remove
prompting, which is right for a scratch worktree and wrong for a stream you
come back to over weeks — a stray "remove" at the end of a session would take
the work with it. `.claude/worktrees/` remains fine for short-lived scratch
worktrees.

Two traps this creates, because `EnterWorktree` does not follow the convention:

- It **defaults to the in-repo location**, so a worktree created that way lands
  in the wrong place and has to be moved.
- It **auto-names the branch `worktree-<name>`**, which violates the `feat/…`
  naming too. Rename after creating.

Before concluding a branch has no worktree, check **both** locations — the
split is what made the picture feel scattered in the first place.
