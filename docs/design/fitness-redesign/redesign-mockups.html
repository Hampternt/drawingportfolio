Desktop keeps the phone's two-step promise by swapping camera for keyboard: ⌘K from anywhere, type, Enter logs the default portion into the current meal slot. No barcode scanner, no separate add screen.

2Scan → confirm → logged: the four-tap path

Your flow, drawn out. A known product is two taps from home screen to logged (Scan, then Log) because every food carries a default portion. A never-seen product costs one extra step — set the usual portion once, and it is two taps forever after. Editing the portion and cancelling to library-only are both present but demoted, so the common case is never slowed by the rare one.
