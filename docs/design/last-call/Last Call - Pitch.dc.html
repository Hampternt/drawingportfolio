Losing badly is always fixable — you just have to drink for it. Finishing the can is the comeback button, and the whole table watches you press it.

You drink because you chose to

Never as a punishment for losing. Drinking is what buys you the fun stuff.

Nobody waits their turn

Up to eight people all play at the same time, then it resolves together.

It balances itself

The most aggressive player is the drunkest one. Nobody has to police anything.

How a round actually goes
Three to five minutes · everyone plays at once
Draw 20s

Bin what you don't want. Finish the can for a fresh hand, or keep what you have.

Deal

Tonight's rule change flips face up for everyone.

Diplomacy 3 min cap

Talk. Deal, threaten, lie, show a card. Nothing said here is binding, which is the point.

Lock 60s

Cards and targets, in secret. Everything flips at once when the last player locks.

Respond 20s

You can see what's aimed at you. Twenty seconds to block, redirect, or scream.

Resolve

Damage lands, everyone drinks what they spent.

Your phone is your hand, and only you see it. The TV — if there is one — shows the bars, the clock, and who hasn't locked in. Knocked out? You become a ghost: one vote a round to add damage to someone else's attack, so you're still in it.
