Modules

Every reusable piece in Last Call, with the values to build it from. Each module states its anatomy, its data, its states and where it appears. Rules live in Design Doc v2; the rendered screens are in Game UI. This file is the bridge between them — if a number here and a number in Game UI disagree, Game UI is the source of truth.

A · Foundations

A.1 Deck colour is the app's only taxonomy

Deck identity drives every colour decision. There is no separate card-type palette, no per-player colour. Two ramps: fill for solid areas (pips, plaque rules, dots) and ink for anything sitting on the dark ground (label text, borders, cost bars). They differ for Wine only, which is too dark to read as text.

A.2 Surfaces, type, motion
  • Ground #0B0910 page, #0E0C14 device, #16121F/#17141F cards, #251F35 raised card, #2E2742 focused card.
  • Hairlines rgba(242,238,248,.1–.28). Deck-tinted borders use the ink hex at 59–99 alpha suffix.
  • Text #F2EEF8 primary, #CDC6DD body, #A79FBB secondary, #8D87A0 label, #6A6480 faint.
  • Type Archivo 800/900 for names, counts and numerals (tracking −.02 to −.035em); Space Grotesk 400/500 for card body; IBM Plex Mono 500 for machine values (times, room codes, deck names, counters).
  • Micro-caps Space Grotesk 700 at 8–11px, letter-spacing .12–.14em, uppercase, #8D87A0.
  • Card lift 0 Npx 0 rgba(5,3,10,.5) hard offset (N = 3 small, 6 focused) plus a soft drop. This is the deck-of-cards look; do not replace it with a blur-only shadow.
  • Motion 190ms for state, 280ms for position, 130ms for taps — all cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.3,1). Flights are the exception (E.1). prefers-reduced-motion MUST stop flights and hold them at rest opacity.

Rule. Colour carries deck. Weight carries importance. Nothing carries state by hue alone — armed, active and focused all also change size, border or position.

C · Hand modules (phone)

C.1 HandWheel — the primary interaction

A 3D cylinder of CardFaces on the X axis. One card in focus at full size, its neighbours receding above and below. The whole element is the drag surface — there is no handle and no scrollbar to hit.

Geometry

STEP   = 21deg per card
RADIUS = 470px
CARD_H = 176px
track: translateZ(-RADIUS)
stage: perspective 1400px
card:  rotateX(-d×STEP) translateZ(RADIUS)

Pushing the track back by the radius is what keeps the focused card at its authored size — without it the projection magnifies it. d is the signed, wrapped distance in cards from the focus.

Behaviour
  • Endless. Index wraps both ways; there is no first or last card and no end-stop. Hand size can change without the component caring.
  • Drag at 0.28deg per px of pointer travel, pointer captured, touch-action: none. Downward drag moves toward lower index.
  • Release snaps to the nearest multiple of STEP. Wheel/trackpad steps one card per notch. Both animate with a cubic ease-out over 200–280ms.
  • Depth cues. Opacity 1 − 0.48|d|, hidden past |d| > 2.05, z-index descending with distance. Transitions are disabled while dragging and for cards past |d| 1.6, so wrap-around never animates across the screen.
  • Focus styling. Only |d| < 0.5 gets the 2px deck-ink border, #2E2742 ground and the deep lift; everything else is a 1px hairline on #251F35.
C.2 ArmedColumn

62px fixed column on the left of the wheel. Micro-cap header ARMED n in violet, then one CardMini per armed card, then one dashed 46px empty slot as an affordance. Cards leave the wheel when armed and return when disarmed — the wheel's index list MUST be recomputed, not just filtered visually.

States

Empty (header + one slot) · partial · locked (column dims to 60%, header reads LOCKED n, no slot).

C.3 CostRail

26px column on the right. A flat sequence of 3px bars, right-aligned: one bar per point of cost, 2px gap within a card, 7px gap between cards, coloured by deck ink. The active card's bars are 14px wide at full opacity; the rest are 9px at 40%. Above it sits the card number in mono 9px.

It is a scrubber, a hand census and a cost histogram in 26px: how many cards, how expensive, from which decks, and where you are. Tapping a group jumps the wheel to that card.

Rule. The wheel, the armed column and the rail are one widget in three parts and always ship together. Nothing in this group may require a tap target under 44px, and nothing may depend on precise aim — that constraint is the reason the design exists.

D · Player and table modules

D.1 PlayerPlaque — big screen

204px wide, #16121F, 1px deck border at 66 alpha, 3px deck rule on top, r10, hard lift. Three stacked rows:

  1. Identity — name Archivo 900/22px left, HP Archivo 900/28px right. HP is the largest thing on the plaque.
  2. Drinks — one 8px dot per vessel (3px gap) then the deck names joined by + in mono 11px; the round's draw count as a deck-tinted badge on the right.
  3. Hand strip (D.3) — separated by a hairline, 9px above and below.

Multi-deck: a dot per vessel; the 3px top rule splits 50/50 between the two deck fills. States: idle · locked (violet tick by the name) · drawing (deck rule pulses) · hit (HP flashes rose, plaque shakes 4px) · eliminated (whole plaque to 40%, HP replaced by GHOST).

D.2 SeatRing

The felt: an ellipse inset 40×26 from the stage, r280, radial ground #272038 → #100C1B, 11px rail #2A2340, plus a second hairline ellipse inset a further 56px. Plaques are absolutely positioned on that inner ellipse and centred on their own midpoint.

Seats are laid out by angle from seat 0 at bottom-centre, going clockwise, so the local player is always nearest the viewer. 7 seats is the ceiling for one ring; 8 compresses the two bottom positions.

The same module drives the phone mini-table (F.1) at roughly 0.19 scale, with plaques swapped for name/HP chips.

D.3 HandStrip — hand size without a number

Overlapping 16×24 CardBacks, −4px margin between them, cycling through the player's deck colours so a two-deck hand reads as two-deck. Exact count in mono 10px, right-aligned.

n ≤ 8 → n backs
n > 8 → 7 backs + “+{n−7}” in Archivo 900/13px

D.4 DeckStack & DiscardSlot

Stack: 68×92 CardBack with one offset shadow card behind it at +3px, remaining count in Archivo 900/22px deck ink, deck name in micro-caps beneath. Discard: identical footprint but a dashed hairline border, no grid, neutral #CDC6DD count — it is a destination, not a deck.

Five stacks plus the discard sit centred on the felt with a 14px gap. Under five cards the count turns amber; at zero the stack shows RESHUFFLE and the discard empties into it at the next beat 1.

E · Motion modules

E.1 CardFlight — every card movement in the game

One component with two directions and two scales. A CardBack (big screen) or CardDot (phone) is positioned at the source, given the delta to its destination as CSS custom properties, and animated on a single keyframe track.

--dx / --dy = destination − source
0%   opacity 0, scale .5, at source
3%   opacity 1, scale .85
22%  translate(dx,dy), scale 1
27%  opacity 0 — absorbed
rest: idle until the cycle repeats

  • Draw = deck pile → plaque, in that deck's colour. Stagger 0.2–0.3s per player so seven simultaneous draws read as a burst, not a blur.
  • Discard = plaque → discard slot, same track, later delay.
  • Flights live in a single pointer-events: none layer above the felt and below nothing.
  • Arrival MUST tick the destination's counter — the number and the animation are one event, never two.
E.2 Phase banner

Beat name in Archivo 900/30px (big screen) or 26px (phone), with Round n · beat i of 6 in micro-caps beside it. Each beat owns a hue: Draw amber, Diplomacy mint, Lock violet, Reveal azure, Resolve rose. On change the word cross-fades over 280ms; the hue does not animate.

E.3 Beat timer

A 2px rail under the banner, filling down over the beat's duration in the beat's hue. Under 5s it turns rose. No numerals, no ticking — a bar is legible across a room and does not create urgency in a game that is supposed to be slow.

F · Shells

F.1 PhoneShell — 390×844

Fixed vertical order, every screen, no exceptions:

  1. Status row 40px — time and room code, mono 12px.
  2. Phase banner (E.2) with the round/beat line right-aligned.
  3. Tabs — HAND / TABLE / LOG, 800/11px caps, 9×14×8 padding, 2px underline on the active tab — violet #B48EF7 for HAND, azure #6FB6FF for TABLE, #8D87A0 for LOG — hairline under the row. Order never changes and the active tab is never hoisted; only colour and underline move. Top of screen, not bottom.
  4. View — flexes to fill.
  5. Action bar — 12×14×18 padding, 64px primary buttons r8. Two primaries share a row when the beat offers a choice; the drinking option is always the amber one.

The thumb zone belongs to the beat's decision. Navigation lives at the top precisely because it is not what you reach for mid-round.

F.2 BigScreenShell — 1920×1080

88px header (wordmark, room code, round, then the phase banner right-aligned), then the felt filling everything below it. Read from 3–4 metres: nothing under 18px, HP and beat name are the two things visible from the doorway.

It is a display, never an input. No hover states, no focus rings, no controls — every affordance belongs to a phone.

F.3 MiniTable — phone TABLE tab

The same felt at 466px tall for rooms with no TV. Centre column: a 46×62 draw pile above a 112px list of Event / Quest / Discard rows (7px dot, micro-cap label, Archivo 900/13px count). Seats become name/HP chips; flights become CardDots (B.5). Same data, same events, one order of magnitude less detail.

Rule. Big screen and mini-table render the same state object and receive the same events. If a thing happens on one and not the other, that is a bug, not a scale decision.
